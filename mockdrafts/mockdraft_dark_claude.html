Centerpiece / Diorama

When It's a Whole Scene, Not Just a Model

Some commissions stop being "a miniature" somewhere around the third piece of resin terrain. Environment studies and full dioramas get their own scope: a rocky ledge, a lighting story, two forces that were never going to share a table peacefully.

This is where the shelf work becomes something closer to a display piece: sculpted basing, layered terrain, and enough contrast in the paint scheme that the story reads before anyone gets close enough to see brushwork.

About

The Guy Behind the Wash

Before the paintbrush, Dave spent several years as a US Army officer, which means the shelf isn't just neat, it's inventoried. Basecoat, wash, highlight, done: a standard operating procedure that hasn't changed much since Cadia, except now the only casualties are chipped rims and the occasional dry-brush accident.

Off the bench he's more often on a rock face than a recliner. Route-finding and squad tactics turn out to share a lot of vocabulary: read the terrain, commit to a line, trust the anchor. It also explains the small-parts patience — nobody free-solos a Bloodletter's wing joints.

Requisition Manifest

Commission Tiers

Three standard tiers of sanctioned work. Ballpark planning numbers, not a binding quote until Dave's seen reference photos and a model count.

Tier I

Tabletop Ready

$40–70/ model

  • Three-color scheme plus wash
  • Basic edge highlights
  • Standard basing
  • Reads clean at arm's length across the table

Tier II

Display Quality

$90–160/ model

  • Layered blending and glazes
  • Freehand insignia or battle damage
  • Upgraded basing materials
  • Holds up to a close-up photo

Tier III

Centerpiece / Diorama

Custom quotestarting ~$250+

  • Multi-model narrative scenes
  • NMM, OSL, sculpted terrain
  • Optional display-case build
  • Scoped after a reference-photo review

Every quote is scoped to the actual model and reference photos, not a price list read off a shelf. Rush timelines and heavy conversion work run above these ranges.

Commission

Get Something Painted

Have a squad, a warband, or one very important centerpiece model gathering dust in a box? Commission inquiries are open. The more detail up front, the faster the quote comes back.

Email Dave

Include in your message

  • Army / faction Astra Militarum regiment, Chaos god, whatever's on the shelf.
  • Model count A single centerpiece or a full squad, roughly how many models.
  • Reference images Box art, existing scheme photos, or a Pinterest board, whatever exists.
  • Timeline Tournament deadline, gift date, or just "whenever it's right."

No online form yet, just a real inbox. Expect a reply within a few days with a ballpark quote and a rough queue date.
